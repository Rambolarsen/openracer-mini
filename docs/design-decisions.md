# Design decisions

## Accepted direction
Two variants: Mini 3 optimized around HDZero AIO15, Mini 3.5 around Gamma and separate Race V3. Prototype Mini 3 first. Share principles and parametric architecture; identical physical parts across variants are desirable only if validated. Prioritize racing performance, durability, repairability and low mass.

## Open decisions
Arm retention architecture; exact motor and prop selection; stack and canopy envelope; carbon layup and fasteners; antenna mounting; licensing and project name clearance. Study upstream OpenRacer without copying CAD until license review. Validate by full-scale printed fit checks (not flight parts), carbon prototype, bench tests and controlled flight testing.
