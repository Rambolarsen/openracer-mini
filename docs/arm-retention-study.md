# Arm retention study
Upstream reference: https://github.com/OpenRacer/OpenRacer (README, master, reviewed 2026-11-27). GPL-3.0 listed; no upstream geometry imported. README describes sandwich plates, through-bolts, pressnuts and separate arm-locking screws for smaller stacks. Its 30 mm stack bolts also retain arms. Upstream 5-inch carbon thicknesses are not directly transferable. CAD geometry has not been inspected; contact faces and tolerances remain unknown.

## Candidates
A. Keyed arm root captured between plates, dedicated accessible fastener(s). Preferred for initial exploration: positive anti-rotation, decoupled electronics, possible low part count; risk of stress concentration and plate spreading.
B. Two dedicated bolts per arm in sandwich: simple positive retention, higher hardware mass and possible crowded center.
C. One-piece base: mass and simplicity control sample; fails individual arm replacement requirement.

Prototype A and B before choosing. Check fastener access, arm extraction path, root radius, bearing area, carbon edge distance, plate deflection, motor wire routing and repeatable alignment. Mechanical retention must not rely on friction alone without validation. Motor wires may still need disconnection/desoldering; do not promise solderless repairs until wiring is selected.
