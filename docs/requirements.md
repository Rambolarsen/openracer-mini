# Requirements

## Common
- Dedicated racing, True-X baseline; identical replaceable arms; aim to replace arms without removing electronics.
- 25.5 mm nominal AIO mounting; verify actual hole size, outline, connector and component keepouts.
- Underslung battery, XT30 candidate, protected camera and antennas, airflow, strain relief and electrical isolation from conductive carbon.
- Parametric CAD, STEP, DXF and STL exports with revision identifiers.

| Parameter | Mini 3 | Mini 3.5 |
|---|---|---|
| Props | 76.2 mm | 88.9 mm |
| Wheelbase | 116 mm | 136 mm |
| Arm width | 7 mm | 9 mm |
| Arm thickness | 3 mm | 4 mm |
| Motor mounting candidate | 9 mm | 12 mm |
| Power | 3S | 4S |

Targets only. True-X nominal adjacent motor spacing is wheelbase/sqrt(2), giving approximately 5.8 mm and 7.3 mm tip gap respectively; validate actual prop tolerance, deflection and canopy clearance. Validate current draw, battery voltage, temperatures and mounting against actual components before flight.
